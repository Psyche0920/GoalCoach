你好 is a basic Mandarin greeting, conventionally translated as Hello.
The dictionary-tone pinyin is nǐ hǎo. Spoken third-tone sandhi changes the first syllable in connected speech.
A short example exchange is A: 你好！ B: 你好！
These notes do not define an official syllabus or claim educational accreditation.
